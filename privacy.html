EDEN Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

EDEN Uploader (shown on Google's consent screen as "cloud code") is a private desktop tool. Its only user is its owner, Sos Terdzhanian, who uses it to upload videos he made to his own YouTube channels through the YouTube API Services.

By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to the data Google processes.

What the tool accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl), the tool uploads videos the owner selects and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist and scheduled publish time. It reads the channel name and ID and the status of the videos it uploaded. It does not read comments, messages, subscribers, analytics or any other user's data.

What it stores

The OAuth tokens and a log of uploaded video IDs and titles are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and no data is sent anywhere except to Google's APIs.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Deleting the tool's local .secrets/youtube folder removes the stored tokens; the upload log is deleted on request within 7 days.
